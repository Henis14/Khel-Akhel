DARAS • Khel-Akhel Studio

All that comes, comes in play.

All that flows, flows. All that goes, goes. All is One.

Khel-Akhel is a creative journey where art takes many forms, shaped by curiosity, experimentation, and the natural flow of making. There is no fixed beginning or end, only an unfolding. A play across forms, materials, and ideas that becomes a way of seeing—bringing together art, objects, spaces, and experiences as they emerge.

It is an invitation to play, explore, and connect. All are welcome to look, interact, participate, immerse, reflect, and perhaps discover something new along the way.
